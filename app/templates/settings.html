A certificate (with its chain, if it has one, the server's own first) and its private key, both PEM files; the key without a passphrase. It must carry the name or address you type into the browser. NXray checks they belong together and starts using them at once, with no restart.

To turn on built-in HTTPS, set NXRAY_ENABLE_HTTPS=true in nxray.env, make sure the 8443:8443 line is in the ports of docker-compose.yml, and run docker compose up -d. NXray then makes a self-signed certificate (upload your own here afterwards) and answers on https://<this machine>:8443.
